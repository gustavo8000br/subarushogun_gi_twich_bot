# OPS-6 Risk Assessment

[Português brasileiro](../../pt-BR/qa/assessments/OPS-6-risk-20261007.md)

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-07
**Scope:** Panel runtime status, localized next actions, empty states, operational readability, responsive layout, keyboard focus.

| Risk | Initial likelihood × impact | Evidence / mitigation | Residual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Runtime status is overwritten by localization or displays stale/API-owned content as product copy | 3 × 4 | Dynamic values are rendered after applying catalogs; integration contracts and real `/health`/Chrome inspection verify the current version, DB/Twitch state, and ping in pt-BR, English, and Spanish. | Low |
| Empty-state or eligibility guidance routes the streamer to an unavailable operation | 3 × 3 | Pure navigation tests cover disconnected, eligible, ineligible, and existing-queue states; the connected/ineligible live panel was inspected. | Low |
| Small viewport hides controls or causes horizontal page overflow | 4 × 3 | The initial QA failure at 320 px was reproduced by a new test and fixed; all eight pages were checked at 320/768/1280 px across three locales (72/72 fit). | Low |
| Keyboard users cannot identify focused navigation | 3 × 4 | Browser keyboard review found `outline-style: none`; a regression test failed before the correction, and the final built panel reports `outline-style: solid` at 2 px. | Low |
| UI change leaks secrets or changes Twitch points | 1 × 5 | Changes are confined to panel presentation, catalogs, and navigation; no API or financial operation behavior changed. Static analysis returned zero findings; no secret was displayed and no Twitch write was performed. | Low |

The two QA findings were resolved test-first and verified against the rebuilt Compose service. No open high-severity risk remains in this story. The connected Twitch channel is ineligible for Channel Points; reward, chat, and OAuth writes were intentionally not exercised.
