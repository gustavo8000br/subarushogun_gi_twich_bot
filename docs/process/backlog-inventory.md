### Criteria

**Priority**

- **P0:** on the currently workable critical path of OPS-15, or a rule contradiction that can cause a wrong action or commit. Do now.
- **P1:** needed before the OPS-15 cutover, an owner-reported defect, or code already written that only awaits its gate. Do next.
- **P2:** approved follow-up with its own story, blocked by P0/P1 items or waiting for an owner input; also optimizations with measured or estimated gain.
- **P3:** gated by an external condition (release stage `rc`, stable UI, refinement) or low value. Do not start.

**Difficulty** (remaining effort, my judgment by these criteria, not measured)

- **P (small):** up to half a day, one area, no extra planning gate.
- **M (medium):** one to three days, one test-first slice, at most one specialist gate (UX or data) plus the @qa gate.
- **G (large):** several slices or more than one specialist gate (UX, data, architecture); several days.
- **GG (very large):** declared COMPLEX (score 16 or more of 25) with three or more gates and several slices.

For owner items, difficulty is the effort of the person, not of the agents.
